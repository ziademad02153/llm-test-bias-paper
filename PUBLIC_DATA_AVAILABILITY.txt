The supplementary public package contains all 900 recorded condition outcomes across 225 matched trials on 15 tasks and the R scripts and outputs needed to reproduce the statistical analyses. Source implementations, task specifications and generated test suites are supplied for nine tasks with documented licenses. Source-bearing artifacts for six tasks (REAL_TASK_004, 006, 010, 011, 019 and 020) are withheld pending clarification of redistribution permission. Their numerical outcomes and source repository/commit pointers remain included. Consequently, the public package supports reanalysis of the full study but does not support complete re-execution of test evaluation for the six withheld tasks. No numerical outcomes were removed or recalculated for this release.
